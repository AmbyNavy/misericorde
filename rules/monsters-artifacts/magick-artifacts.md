# Magick Artifacts

As the Referee, constructing a magick item can be a simple matter of enhancing some ordinary thing, like a +1 sword or lock pick. There is nothing wrong with such ideas, but you may be better off saying that “plus things” are items of exceptional quality—not magick. Make them available for an appropriate amount of crowns and adjust your foes and obstacles accordingly. Maybe you can turn the magick lock in your adventure into a superior lock that requires a *superior* lock pick? When magick items are rare, they become desirable and wondrous. If you make them common, their magick risks not feeling magical. 

On the following pages are concepts to develop further, and then some fleshed out examples. The point is—just like with monsters— to both give you some material to work with and to demonstrate ways of working with it. 

Remember that an item may draw HP, and that cursed items are dangerous to use and hard to identify or discard (p. 65).
