# Monster Attributes, Task Rolls & Morale

Monsters and non-player characters don’t have attribute scores. For task rolls, auctions and contests, use av for what you think are the monster’s good attributes and SV or even raw HD for what you think are poor ones. Do the same for high and poor morale values. Some monsters can have one or more very low attributes compared to a human. How smart are rocs or zombies? Set such attributes to one.
