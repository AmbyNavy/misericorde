# Optional Rules

*Where the reader is given tools to extend the game in regard to various play-styles, genres, settings and campaigns.*

*Whitehack* can be a versatile game system. In addition to traditional fantasy, the previous editions have been used for such disparate things as imperial space opera, nineteenth century grand tours, cowboy vampires in mechas, cosmic horror, anthropomorphic cyberpunk and the gritty struggle against chaos in alternative Renaissance Europe.

The dissociation between classes and vocations, and the way abilities and miracles work, go a long way towards making the game adaptable, but this chapter presents further options. There are also Referee specific tools in the ensuing chapters.
