# Character Identity & Save

Is the name you started with still suitable? Do you want to make notes about age, gender or something else at this point? The character’s identity, background and social situation will recursively grow in play. 

Add color to your character’s save by giving it a **double-edged** modulation: a word or phrase like “Gambler”, “Old”, “Not on my watch”, “Paranoia”, “My Destiny!” or “Guardian Ancestor.” This affects some of the character’s saves (p. 48), either improving or diminishing her chances. It also adds character. You may change it at levels three, six and nine.
