# Loot Rolls

Loot rolls can be used when you are *unsure* what—if anything—a monster has on them or in a nearby stash. Add HD to any modification listed in the LT column. Roll against that total as in a task roll.

- Failures and fumbles render loot according to the below loot table, of a *maximum total worth* no higher than the monster’s xp value, counted in crowns.[^1]
-  Success on the other hand gives you roughly that maximum, *plus* something extra that isn’t limited in that way. 
- Crits let you read from the line below (if there is one) on the loot table.

| LT  | Category | Failure                      | Success      |
| :-: | -------- | ---------------------------- | ------------ |
| A   | Animal   | Nothing                      | Food.        |
| R   | Regular  | Mundane and expected         | Valuable.    |
| M   | Monster  | Valuable but still expected. | Rare.        |
| H   | Hoard    | Valuable, rare. Magick.      | Artifact(s). |
{: .compact-table .content-sized-table }

The exact loot is always up to the Referee, but players can make suggestions. All loot generated in this way should be plausible, but when the table says “expected” it means just that: you could more or less count on a certain piece of loot to be there (e.g. “coat,” “boots”). 

Use the random input tables (p. 107) to get ideas for the type and origin of a particular item, as well as interesting complications and history tied to it. If you, for example, roll rare monster loot on the above table, and then use input tables A, C, D, H and F getting “Jewel,” “Elven,” “Ambition,” “Deal” and “Pass,” you could maybe decide that the rare loot is a jewel once belonging to noble elven exiles, who payed it as a fee when fleeing through a mountain pass after a failed political coup. What meaning does the jewel carry in elven society? Are the original owners or their relatives still alive? Such questions can be answered in future adventures, generated with the random loot as the original seed.

[^1]: Most of the time, it will be less. For example, far from every Fighter is going to have things worth 90 crowns on them.
