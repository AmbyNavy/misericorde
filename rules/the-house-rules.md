# The House Rules

All of the following rules apply to Thanaera, my home game—if you're a player in that game, welcome! Everything below is described as best I can.

**[+]** is used as shorthand for double positive rolls, and **[-]** for double negative.

## Character Creation

The Strong and the Deft are available from [Character Classes](chargen/classes/index.md) - magic options are listed below. [Rare character classes](optional-rules/rare-character-classes/index.md) are available upon request to use.

All classes add their base level 1 HD to all their HD totals each level. HD rolls under your current total stay the same instead of going down—you can never say I’m not nice.

### Mage - the Conscious

The Conscious has an Æther pool equal to a roll of their level’s current HD. It is tracked seperately and is what is consumed by casting miracles. Excess spent Æther is deducted from HP at a 2:1 ratio. Instead of the extra inactive miracles, the Conscious gets extra Æther equal to their level for an intelligencel of 13+, or double their level for 16+.

The Conscious picks one Core at character creation, and within that core, they pick a Channel. Two miracles for that Channel are to be made as their initially slotted miracles. When a new slot is earned, the character can choose to study or branch out in their magic for both their active and inactive miracle.

- **Study:** Develop a new miracle in a channel you have access to. 
- **Branch:** Take one miracle in a new channel within your core. Can only be done if the character has three miracles in all their currently available channels.

The Conscious does not supernaturally heal, and otherwise functions as the Wise.

#### Cores, Channels, Descriptions

##### Time Core

- **Past:** Retrieved information, reversed damages, slowed processes.
- **Present:** Stall the clock, maintain the effects, preserve the state.
- **Future:** To accelerate action, to foresee and predict, to change a course.

##### Matter Core[^1]

- **Creation:** Converting latent Æther into physical matter and energy.
- **Modification:** Manipulating the properties and traits of physical matter.
- **Destruction:** Annihilating physical matter and Æther energies.

##### Space Core

- **Dimension:** Definition and manipulation of the vertices of the physical world.
- **Translocation:** Locational modification of objects and beings within a space.
- **Perception:** Adjustment to the perception of a space, or the senses of a being.

### Host - the Dreaming

Instead of paying HP costs to perform Miracles, use scrolls, or any other item that draws HP, Dreaming characters instead accumulate Twist. Twust represents the accumulated fatigue, strain, and internal diminishment from manipulating magical forces. If a character’s Twist is ever equal to or greater than their current HP, either because their Twist increased or because they lost HP, they must Save vs Death or take their current Twist total as damage (which can be lethal!).

Dreaming characters remove half of their Twist with a night’s rest, removing it fully if they succeed at a constitution task roll. 

The personality of the Dreaming will shift over time to be more similar to that of the crux of their psychic chain. The person at the top of a psychic chain, on becoming a psychot to someone, has three **tenets** defined for them, which pass down to the psychots beneath in more strong manner over time. For example, Ichó Agios Kallistrate Prosechtikós has the tenets of Order, Protection and Leadership, meaning her hosts will shift towards these personality traits becoming dominant.

Over time, the Dreaming selects two minor quirks, one moderate quirk, one major quirk and then one or more extreme quirks. There may be examples listed for the individual they are becoming a host of, but broadly one can consider quirks that fall within the tenets of their crux. The table below shows how long it takes the effects of each strength of quirk to set in, and when they do.

| Quirk strength | Starts to set in               | Time to take full effect |
| -------------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| Gentle         | Three months of being a host.† | One month                |
| Noticeable     | One year of being a host.      | Three months             |
| Invasive       | Two years of being a host.     | Six months               |
| Consuming      | Every decade of being a Host.  | One year                 |

*† - at three months in, pick two Gentle quirks. Each takes one month, one after the other - you choose the order.* 

Try and pick personality quirks from differing Tenets, and ones you would find interesting to explore and roleplay - the fun is in the change! You can always speak with your referee to come up with a quirk that isn't on the example list.  

Slotting a miracle outside of your tenets makes it two steps more costly. The Dreaming otherwise works as per The Wise.

## Species Group Bonuses

### Human (no species group)

It’s never too hard to find siblings in arms to help you in your cause. You’ll remember the face of each one that you see die, and forget those who move on in life.

### Ælith

On a **Critical** or **Fumble**, you may ask your Warden a question, which will be answered truthfullyah [^2]. **Fumbles** will often be cryptic or short, while **Criticals** are kinder and clearer.

The right people will buy the revelations you have for high prices. The wrong people will beg you for truths and guidance in their trying times. The act of giving either feels amoral to you. 

### Fellfolk

On a **Critical**, heal HP equal to your level. This can increase your current Vigour above its normal limit, and can stack.

When you exert your legacy, lineage and history upon other non-fellfolk, they feel more compelled to give you what you want. They don’t like it - nobody does. Not even you.

### Silvite

On a **Fumble**, your next task roll gains **[+]**.

You have a modular body and internal function that allows you to find and replace parts of yourself with other rarer or more specialised Silvite models. You feel strongly about the current assembly of your body.

### Orukin

On a **Critical**, you spot a weakness in an construction or person of your Warden’s choice. Tests you make exploiting or protecting that weakness gain **[+]**. **Fumbles** instead reveal inconvenient weaknesses.

Your enemies falter faster in the face of your violence, other than fellfolk, who fight to the bitter end. You’re rarely capable of pulling punches until the enemy breaks - your well of regret over this only grows.

### Naturan

On a **Critical**, you notice any traps, passageways, beings or treasures that are concealed and nearby.

There’s always a good rest spot around somewhere in the wilderness, that’ll be safe for you and your friends - it just takes a bit of focused seeking. One day, you won’t want to leave that spot, and you will be reclaimed by Her.


## Additional Rules

Scrolls cannot be crafted. I do not yet have an alchemy system I like.
