# Terminology

- **Ability:** Some kind of trait that lets a character do something unusual. Each class has special abilities.  
- **Adventure:** The Referee’s play material.  
- **Affiliations:** The character’s contacts, such as organizations or powerful beings. 
- **Attack rolls:** Rolling dice to attack someone in combat. Roll under or equal to your attack value (av), but over the opponent’s defense score (df), in order to hit.
- **Attribute:** There are six default character attributes—Strength, Agility, Toughness, Intelligence, Willpower, and Charisma. Optionally, this array can be replaced or modified to better suit the setting, campaign or even individual character.  
- **Auctions:** Longer contests where you make bids, trying to maximize your chances to win. They can be used for many things, from chases to cinematic fights against hordes of lesser enemies.  
- **AV:** Attack Value.  
- **Base:** A headquarters, larger vehicle, leader or similar, shared by the party of adventurers.  
- **Campaign:** A series of related adventures.  
- **CL:** Corruption level. In some campaigns, you may be subject to radiation, insanity, the influence of chaos, etc.  
- **Class:** The character type, and also the player–world interface.  
- **Contests:** Contests are resolved through opposite task rolls.  
- **Crits:** Rolling your value exactly in a task or attack roll means a spectacular success.  
- **DF:** Defense—how hard you are to harm.  
- **Double rolls:** Sometimes you roll two dice and pick the best or the worst of the outcomes (positive or negative double rolls, respectively).  
- **Fumbles:** Rolling 20 in a task or attack roll (and some saves) means a terrible failure.  
- **Groups:** Each character belongs to groups that define her. There are three kinds: species, vocations and affiliations.  
- **HD:** Harm Dice. Roll the harm dice to obtain harm points (HP). Player characters roll all of the dice at each new level. If the new result is lower, you may keep the previous one. There is no such thing as a wasted harm die.  
- **HP:** Harm Points. A measure of your ability to stay in harm’s way. Harm points are used mainly in combat, to perform miracles and when using magick items.  
- **Harm Cost:** The cost in harm points for dealing with the arcane, when performing a miracle.  
- **Inactives:** Abilities you have but cannot currently use.  
- **Initiative:** Roll a d6 to go first in combat.  
- **Level:** The character’s level of experience.  
- **Macro:** A setting entity or phenomenon—usually on a very large scale—represented as a character.  
- **Miracle:** A magick effect of some sort.  
- **Miracle Wording:** A phrasing of a miracle, such as “Magick Light” or “Voice of Doom,” that is the basis of what you can do with it.  
- **Modus roll:** When the adventure enters a new phase, the Referee can roll for unexpected circumstances requiring a different modus operandi.  
- **MV:** Move. The distance someone moves in ten seconds. This is usually counted in feet, but in some contexts it may be counted in grid squares (units of 5 × 5 feet).  
- **Quality:** The face value of the die in a successful roll.  
- **Phase:** A standard section of an adventure.  
- **Rare Class:** A restricted, rarely available class.  
- **Round:** A ten second period of time.  
- **SV:** A value describing how weathered your are, used to save yourself from some threat.  
- **Setting:** Where the adventures take place.  
- **Slots:** Spaces for a character’s class abilities.  
- **Special attacks and combat options:** Special ways of attacking, such as tripping someone or feinting.  
- **Species:** Dwarf, elf etc. Without an explicit species group, the character is in the game world’s species norm group.  
- **Species Class:** A species with predefined characteristics, vulnerabilities, resistances etc., often with restrictions of class.  
- **Task rolls:** Roll a d20 under or equal to an attribute score, to succeed at a challenging task.  
- **Trained roll:** Task rolls that require training.  
- **Turn:** Your turn to act in a combat round order (also an old school time unit).  
- **Vocation:** The character’s calling.  
- **XP:** Experience points to rise in level.
