# Magick Item Concepts

- **Antidote Potion.** Allow an extra, double positive save against poison.
- **Boots of Age.** Old wearers appear young. Young wearers appear old.
- **Boots of the Bard.** No magick can hold you. The boots hum softly.
- **Circlet of Leadership.** Make double positive rolls when dealing with crowds.
- **Crow Hat (c)[^1].** Owner is constantly followed by 3d6 crows.
- **Dead Language Quill.** Can write any dead language regardless of writer knowledge.
- **Demon Contract.** Read this scroll to bind a known demon for 10 minutes. Be careful not to misread anything.
- **Dipped Flask.** Contains a huge amount of water—which runs from an actual lake or stream.
- **Disease Transfuser.** Cure one person by transferring the disease to another.
- **Door Detection Device.** Apparatus with a glass bulb that blinks 3 ft from doors.
- **Dragon Armor (c).** DF 8. Save or stop for close examination when you see gold.
- **Dust of Appearance.** Sprinkle to reveal all invisible creatures or objects.
- **Elven Chainmail.** Disregard extra HP costs for performing miracles in armor.
- **Flute of Withering.** All plants within 100 ft wither. Knocks out plant creatures.
- **Glove of Iron Control.** Control movement of up to 12 hd enemies in sight, if they wear plate mail.
- **Guiding Sphere.** A hovering sphere of soft light that moves towards an exit.
- **Healing Potion.** Heal d6+1 damage.
- **Insectoid Gloves (c).** These gloves stick to everything.
- **Instamagick Reflector.** Point it at something to create an image on paper. 24 charges.
- **Jaded Figurine (c).** Save each day or be totally blasé about every- thing.
- **Krugal’s Map (c).** Treasure map. A rainstorm follows the owner.
- **Light Eater.** Cricket construct, eats light in 20 ft radius, can spew it out.
- **Mind Projector.** You can hear the thoughts of others—and they can hear yours.
- **Ogre Bandana (c).** You get Str 18 and Int 6.
- **Potion of Gigantism (c).** Save or gain an inch of height every day.
- **Potion of Liquid Floor.** Crush on floor to turn it liquid for 1 round.
- **Potion of Monster Love.** The first monster you see follows you around for 3 days.
- **Potion of No Shadow (c).** Your shadow disappears into the Shadow Realm.
- **Protective Beetle.** Crawls into ear, giving magick resistance for 10 minutes.
- **Regenerative Bandage (c).** Return HP at ×2 natural rate. Lost limbs grow ... and grow!
- **Ring of Possession.** Possess target for 10 minutes, save negates.
- **Shadow Goggles.** Put on to see into the Shadow Realm. d6 charges.
- **Silver Spear (c).** 4d6 vs. werewolves, all dogs within a mile bark at the wielder.
- **Smoke Elixir.** Turns the imbiber into smoke for 10 minutes.
- **Staff of Humility (c).** −1 HP cost for miracles, but save or fail any miracle.
- **Sword of Fear.** All, including wielders, must save or cower at the start of battle.
- **Tome of History (c).** Gives historical expertise, which you cannot stop talking about.
- **Torque of Divine Debt (c).** Wearer takes over a divine debt (p. 79).
- **Troll Sword (c).** +2 AV and damage, but the sword emits an incredible stench.
- **True Holy Symbol.** Scares d6 regular skeletons and/or zombies.
- **Stick of the Dead (c).** The resurrected turns into a vampire after three days.
- **Wand of Sound.** Transforms victim into a sound for 10 minutes. d6 charges.
- **Watcher Steel Weapon.** Dispels magick if a contest damage vs. HP cost is won.

[^1]: Concepts marked with '(C)' are cursed.
