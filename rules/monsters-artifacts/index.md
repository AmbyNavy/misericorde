# Monsters & Artifacts

*Where the reader learns to use monster stats, generate loot on the fly, run bosses, flesh out new monstrosities and come up with magick items that can have an impact on the game world.*

There are no detailed descriptions of trusty spells, iconic creatures or well known magick artifacts in *Whitehack*. The game connects instead to the RPG megatext—the approximate and collective canon of fantasy game elements, supplemented and often improved upon by the gaming group's imagination.

In this chapter, you will find rules and stats for monsters[^1] as well as a list of seeds for magic artifacts. Both are for you to flesh out as you see fit. Should you only recognize a few or even none of the table entries, **whatever you imagine from the names and keywords will be fine.**

You will also find some fleshed out examples of non-canon creatures and items. Use them as they are, change them or simply have them serve as inspiration or structures for your own inventions.

## "Importing" Monsters

Try to read rather than import monsters from other sources (p. 68). If you prefer actual *Whitehack* notes, any HD, DF and MV values can be transferred directly, as can immunities, resistances and defining qualities. Special attacks, spells etc. are keywords (next page). To import from traditions with other types of stats and values, use the advice for playing such material (p. 72).

[^1]: “Monster” in this chapter is a catchall for any non-player character. But the creatures that are called monsters in a more narrow sense, and who can have some sort of opinion about it, usually don’t consider themselves as such. Goblins and orcs (for example) won’t win Miss Congeniality awards, but they have their own cultures, norms and factions and should be played accordingly.
