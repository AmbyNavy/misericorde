# The Attribute Scores

The character’s basic attributes are strength, agility, toughness, intelligence, willpower and charisma. Roll 3d6 for each, in order. A clever choice of class and groups can compensate for an unwanted result, often making your character surprisingly unique[^1]. The scores are also raised as the character progresses, and low scores can sometimes be beneficial (p. 41).

- **STR:** Strength lets the character move with more weight and is helpful
in any situation that depends mainly on muscle. Characters in the
Strong class get melee bonuses from high strength.  
- **AGI:** Agility is a combination of speed, coordination and dexterity, useful
in many situations. A score of 13 or more gives you a +1 bonus to
initiative in battle. A score of 16 or more gives you +2.  
- **TGH:** Toughness is a combination of grit and physique. High toughness
gives a better chance of surviving and recovering from damage, a
bit more so for characters in the Strong class.  
- **INT:** Intelligence includes memory, thinking, the ability to understand
difficult concepts and read scrolls. You gain one extra language if
you have an intelligence of 13 or more. You gain two for 16 or more.  
- **WIL:** Willpower determines a character’s intuition, will, focus and aware-
ness. Characters in the Wise class get extra magick abilities if they
have a high willpower score.  
- **CHA:** Charisma gives a character a chance of influencing other people,
key to many social situations. Charisma also affects retainer morale
and is the attribute used to parley (p. 55).

[^1]: Rolling attribute scores is a commonly houseruled part of character generation. A good method for Whitehack is one that 1) gives character generation some replay value, 2) makes it a challenge to match the scores with class and groups, and 3) can produce both average Janes and odd characters.
