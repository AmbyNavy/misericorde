# Using Magick Items

Scrolls, potions, wands and many other magick items can be used by everyone. Some require activation—like a word or gesture— and weapons and ray wands require attack rolls to hit a target. All magick items can draw HP. An examination takes d6 hours and requires a trained intelligence roll, but can reveal what an item does and how much it costs to use. An identification miracle does the same but is immediate. 

## Magick Potions & Powders 

Potions can be quaffed, which requires no skill, or thrown at someone, which is a missile attack or missile area attack roll (p. 57). The same goes for attacks with powders. Crushing a vial up to five feet away is an automatic success. Known substances can be carefully sampled and recognized in 10 minutes. 

## Cursed Objects 

Cursed objects are magick, but with wholly or partially negative effects. They are harder to completely understand—a secret modification can be included by the Referee in the player’s trained identification task roll. For powerful cursed objects, a secret extra hp cost can be added to any identifying magick. Characters can be killed this way. 

Cursed objects often require a save to be rid of. Some of them have bleeding harm point costs, meaning that you may all of a sudden have to pay extra HP, hours or even days after using them.

## Reading Scrolls

Magick scrolls are formalized, singular ways to trigger specific miraculous effects. Everything is set—the effect, its parameters and its cost in HP. 

To use a scroll, you must know the language and succeed at a trained roll for intelligence, modified by the scroll’s hp cost. For example, when trying to read a 1 hp Scroll of Know Item, you must make a trained roll for intelligence −1. If you don’t understand but can pronounce a language, you get an additional −6. 

Many scrolls are however written in ancient versions of the languages that the characters know. Suitable groups for the trained roll could for example be the Scribe, Wizard, Monk, Scholar or Scientist vocations, the Order of the Quill or Witch Cult affiliations or even the Mime or Ur-Elven species. 
 
Scrolls need to be protected, commonly in parchment tubes. Unless otherwise noted, scrolls take 10 minutes to use.

### Reading Scrolls Result Table

| Roll           | Result                                                                     |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Fail           | The miracle doesn’t work. You only pay 1 HP and can try again later.       |
| Fumble         | The miracle doesn’t work. You pay full HP anyway. Save or ruin the scroll. |
| Success        | You pay full HP, the miracle works as intended, and 1 charge is spent.     |
| Double success | You pay full HP, the miracle works as intended, and no charge is spent.    |
| Crit           | The miracle works without HP cost, and no charge is spent.                 |

## Poisons & Drugs

Poisons and drugs can but don’t have to be magick. They do unique things, and benefit more from not being standardized than from having an orderly set of rules. The following parameters can inspire individual descriptions, as shown in the listed examples.

- **Primary Effects** may be partly or wholly avoidable through a save. Beneficial effects usually have no save. 
- **Secondary Effects** usually entail a save regardless of what they are.
- **Remedies** are what is needed to negate one or both of the effects. Not all poisons and drugs have remedies. 
- **Addiction.** If you use a drug more than once, it is common to have to save to avoid addiction. 
- **Withdrawal.** This is what happens when you are addicted and don’t get your accustomed dose.

>**Example Substances**
>
>**Kothic Silver** is a liquid metal from koth cores (named after the explorer Shovo Koth) found in rock creatures. It is legal and commonly sold for 1–10 cr per dose, depending on availability. One dose (or more) yields a −1 HP miracle cost. Save or become addicted to the same number of doses that you have used that day. Fumble the save and take that number of d6s as overdose damage. Abstain and save three days in a row to get clean. Addicts pay double for miracles when in withdrawal.
>**Truth Serum** is a miraculous substance that can be a potion, a powder or even carrier larvæ inserted into the victim’s nose. The effect is considered to be of standard (2 HP) magnitude. Save or be compelled to answer all questions truthfully for 10 minutes. If successful, the truthful time is reduced to 3 rounds. Fumble the save and also take d6 points of damage.
>**Sleeping Gas** is a miraculous area sleep effect in gaseous form, commonly distributed by crushing a fragile glass container in vicinity of the victims. For some reason, the gas tends to have a hue of green. The strength of the gas, the duration of the effect and the negative modification on the victims’ saves vary, with a corresponding variation in buyer price or HP costs for the Wise making it. The baseline is to save or fall asleep for 10 minutes. It is possible to wake a sleeping victim.
>**Goblin Flu** is mistakenly known as a disease. It is in fact a poison from the gomba plant, a seaweed found in underground ponds. It can be concentrated using a trained intelligence roll and distributed in liquids for a dangerous effect (save or take 2d6 damage), but the dried weeds are known to be used by goblins in tea or as tobacco for a mildly refreshing effect. Long term use leads to watery eyes and a running nose, which is probably why this poison has gotten its name.
