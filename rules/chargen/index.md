# Character Generation

*Where the reader either picks a pre-generated character or explores the options involved in creating one from scratch.*

Generating a character in *Whitehack* can be a quick or more involved process, whichever you prefer. If you are in a hurry, just transfer a pre-generated character to a copy of the character sheet. If you have a few more minutes, follow the below steps. If you get stuck, ask the Referee or a co-player for ideas.

1. Pick a preliminary name and roll basic attributes.
2. Pick a character class. If your last character died, your Referee may offer a rare class. These are described in the chapter on Optional Rules (p. 80).
3. Roll the harm dice to get your character’s harm points. Don’t worry about a low score: you get to re-roll at every level, eventually with more dice, and always keep the higher of the new and old scores.
4. Add species, vocation and affiliation groups. If you want a species group, you normally need to pick it now—the other groups can actually wait.
5. Roll starting funds and buy equipment.
6. Color the save and give the character an identity (name, age, gender etc.) of your preference.
