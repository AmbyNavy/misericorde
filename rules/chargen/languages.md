# Languages

All characters are able to understand and speak the most common language as well as the most common language of their own species, both **in the campaign region** of the game world. Usually, this means that humans speak a single language, while characters with a species group also speak a language common to that species. Unless the player has chosen a group that makes it inappropriate (like “barbarian”), all characters are considered literate. If the game world is a pre-industrial historic setting or simply a bit gritty, the Referee may decide that general ability to read and write counts as its own language.

Additional languages come from three regular sources (you can of course also have non-regular ones):

1. A group that the Referee decides comes with a limited language, like Thieves’ Cant for a thieves’ guild or some sacred language for a church. 
2. Bonus languages from high intelligence. Common choices for bonus languages are Draconic, Goblin and dead languages (“High Imperial,” “K’thonian,” etc.) found in old scrolls and books. 
3. Bonus languages from the Clever rare class. 

In all cases, the Referee can decide that a language acquired after level 1 requires time to learn.
